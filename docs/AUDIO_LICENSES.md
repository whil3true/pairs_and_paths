# Audio provenance and licensing register

Provenance register for the user-supplied files now included under `public/assets/audio/` in PR #94. Do not mark unverified third-party source attribution as confirmed.

## Music

- **Forget Me Not** — Kistol. File: `forget_me_not_in_f_major_looped.ogg`. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/forget-me-not . Page declares **CC0 1.0**, commercial use and no mandatory attribution. Preserve this URL and a license snapshot in production documentation.
- **Porcelain Hours** — user-directed music generation using Google Gemini; production file `porcelain_hours.mp3`. **Not CC0.** The owner reports an iterative Gemini conversation with an initial musical output, corrective prompts and multiple revised audio versions. **The exact final-output message and its associated prompts have not yet been inspected.** See [Gemini music provenance ledger](audit/GEMINI_MUSIC_PROVENANCE.md) for the exact committed file fingerprint and a preservation checklist. Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) state that Google does not claim ownership of original content generated by its services; this does **not** automatically guarantee originality, third-party clearance, copyright protection or a perpetual commercial license across every product. Verify applicable product-specific terms and retain dated evidence.

**Verification scope and limitation:** The five normal Git blob matches and
one LFS SHA-256 match provide independent file-level evidence for the
claimed Kenney pack provenance. The official Kenney pages identify the
packs as CC0. Their original ZIP downloads could not be retrieved for
direct archival comparison in this session; the above evidence is a
mirror-based verification, not a claim to have inspected the official ZIPs.
If available later, save original official ZIPs (outside the runtime
`public/` directory), their retrieval date and pack license files.
No SFX bytes have been changed.

As with any CC0 material, licensing does not provide a warranty against
